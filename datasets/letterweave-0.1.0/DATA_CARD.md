# letterweave 0.1.0

Fresh synthetic English emails; machine-labeled, not gold. No private Gmail content or copied upstream corpus is authorized.

855 accepted rows from 900 planned families; 145 source slots excluded. Unknown/failed/QA-excluded rows are omitted from binary-label inputs. All-negative decided vectors are valid.

Labels describe observable purpose and overlap. Review agreement, literal anchors and lexical duplicate checks do not establish semantic accuracy, population prevalence or training suitability. Families and descendants are correlated; intended labels are not gold.
