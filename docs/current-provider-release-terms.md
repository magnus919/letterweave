# Dataset-only publication terms review

Checked official sources October 11, 2026; no provider outreach or account change.

[Nous Portal terms](https://portal.nousresearch.com/terms) cover its inference API. Section 8.4 assigns customer-generated outputs to the customer, conditional on compliance and fees. Section 10.1.4 restricts competing AI/ML products or services; an original generator and public synthetic dataset without hosted inference or model training are not automatically such competition. Sections 10.1.5 and 13.2 require applicable upstream terms. Service-access distribution in 10.2.1 is distinct from publishing customer outputs. No express dataset-only ban or general provider-written-permission requirement was established.

The [official API specification](https://portal.nousresearch.com/api/openapi) and [subscription-proxy guide](https://hermes-agent.nousresearch.com/docs/user-guide/features/subscription-proxy) do not identify the upstream agreement for this exact model route.

[OpenAI Services Agreement](https://openai.com/policies/services-agreement/) sections 4.1 and 3.3(e) address output rights and competing-model development. Its classification exception limits distribution of the resulting models; this project releases data, not a model. Applicability through Nous remains unidentified. TypeSafe's agreement is not established as applicable merely because another route was considered.

Decision for the parent: record applicable route/account terms and assess the actual dataset-only purpose. Do not characterize missing applicability evidence as a proven prohibition or require written permission without a relevant clause. Code-only release uses original code and can remain separate from data publication. No legal clearance or downstream training authorization is claimed.
